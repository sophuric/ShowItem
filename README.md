Commands:

- `/show`, `/showitem` - shows item in your main hand by default
- `/show main|mainhand`
- `/show offhand`
- `/show head|helmet|cap|hat`
- `/show chest|chestplate|tunic`
- `/show legs|leggings|pants`
- `/show feet|boots`

Will say "holding" or "wearing" in chat depending on what slot it's in, and the quantity for stackable items. Additionally, you can hover over the item to see the full item tooltip, i.e enchantments, attack damage.
